Customer Churn Prediction & LTV Engine

End-to-end ML system that predicts customer churn risk and forecasts lifetime value, deployed as a live API and visualized through an interactive BI dashboard.

  • Analyzed churn and LTV for 7,043 telecom customers, comparing Logistic Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest classifiers (Random Forest: 0.84 ROC-AUC, 79% accuracy).
  • Segmented customers into Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum LTV tiers of about 1,760 customers each, with LTV calculated as monthly charges × tenure.
  • Built a 4-page Power BI dashboard with DAX measures reporting a 26.5% churn rate, 2.86M revenue lost to churn (17.8% of the total) and 1,227 high-value customers lost.
  • Found churn highest on month-to-month contracts and in early tenure (47% within the first 12 months, against 7% at 61–72 months), and served the LTV model through a FastAPI endpoint.
